# [Better Trap Selector](https://www.mousehuntgame.com/preferences.php?tab=mousehunt-improved-settings#mousehunt-improved-settings-better-trap-selector)

Adds filters, skin and codex options, and correct base stats to the trap selector.

## Features

- **Random Skin Button**: Adds a button to the skin selector to equip a random skin.
- **Equipped Base in Skin Previews**: Shows your equipped base when previewing skins.

## Options

### General

- **Show quick filters and sort buttons**: Adds one-click power type filters to the Trap and Base selectors, and one-click sorting by Power, Power Bonus, Luck, Attraction Bonus, name, or quantity to the Trap, Base, Charm, and Cheese selectors.
- **Mark items with special effects**: Adds a dot to items with special effects, including ones that only work at your current location.
- **Add a background gradient to your trap**

### Skins

- **Show larger skin images**
- **Show unowned skins**

### Codex

- **Show larger codex images**
- **Codex position**: Show the active codex at the bottom of the trap view, where the game puts it, or hide it.

### Bases

- **Show the real stats for variable bases**: Shows the correct stats for the Prestige, Rift Hailstone Singularity, Denture, and Printing Press bases.
- **Show toothlet and printing paper counts**: Shows how many Ful'mina's Charged Toothlets you have for the Denture bases, and Prolific Printing Papers for the Printing Press bases.
