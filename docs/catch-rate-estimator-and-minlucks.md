# [Catch Rate & Minluck](https://www.mousehuntgame.com/preferences.php?tab=mousehunt-improved-settings#mousehunt-improved-settings-catch-rate-estimate)

See your estimated catch rate, attraction rate, and minluck (guaranteed catch if your luck is higher) for each mouse directly on the Camp page.

## Features

- **Catch Rates**: Shows your estimated catch rate for each mouse with your current trap setup.
- **Minlucks**: Shows the minluck for each mouse available with your current bait.
- **Attraction Rates**: Shows how often each mouse is attracted at your current stage with your current cheese, using rates for your charm when there are any. Shown for locations with attraction rate data.
- **Overall Catch Rate**: A summary row at the bottom shows your overall catch rate, weighted by attraction rate.
- **Sortable Columns**: Click a column header to sort by mouse, attraction rate, minluck, or catch rate. Click it again to reverse the order. Without a sort, the lowest catch rates come first.
- **Refresh Button**: Refresh the table if it's out of date.

## Trap Effectiveness

In [Custom Trap Selector](./custom-trap-selector.md), the trap effectiveness block can be set to rate your trap by the game's own rating or by your overall catch rate. With the overall catch rate, the bar and label colors match the catch rates table: green from 80%, yellow from 40%, and red below that.

More options are available as [feature flags](./feature-flags.md#catch-rate--minluck), including live MHCT attraction rates and a catch rate indicator above the trap view.
