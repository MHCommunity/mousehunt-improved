# [Custom Menu](https://www.mousehuntgame.com/preferences.php?tab=mousehunt-improved-settings#mousehunt-improved-settings-custom-menu)

Rearrange the top menu bar, hide what you don't use, and add shortcuts, toggles, and pinned items.

Click **Edit top menu** in the settings to open the editor. Drag items between the left side, the right side, and the Info bar, or use the arrows. Each item can be shown as an icon, text, or both, or hidden. **Reset to default** puts everything back.

## Items

Besides the game's own menu items, you can add:

- **Links from the game menu**: Most of the Camp, Travel, Inventory, Mice, and other menu links.
- **MouseHunt Improved features**: Location Dashboard, Favorite Setups, Journal Logs, Keyboard Shortcuts, Journal Privacy, Location Timers, Travel Window, the Sidebar, and more. Items for features that are turned off can be turned on and added in one click.
- **Uncaught mice**: The number of mice at your location that you haven't caught yet.
- **Toggles**: Show or hide the location HUD, and turn Dark Mode on or off.
- **Spring Egg Hunt Helper** and the **Adventure Book**.

Some items have extra options, like showing the unread count for the Inbox and Gifts, or only listing setups for your current location in Favorite Setups.

## Info Bar

The Info bar is the line with Hunters Online and Friends Online. Move items into or out of it like any other part of the menu.

## Pinned Items

Pin items to the top menu to use them from any page:

- **Convertibles**: Open them by the quantity you choose.
- **Weapons and bases**: Arm them.
- **Bait and charms**: Arm or disarm them.
- **Potions**: Brew them.
- **Recipes**: Craft them.
- **Items like Scrambles**: Use them.

Group several items under one tab, and show the quantity you have. Items that give an aura, like SB+ supply packs, can show how long the aura has left instead. Quantities update as you use items. If they get out of date, like after a friend sends you supplies, use the refresh button in the editor.

To show full aura times, use the `quick-items-menu-full-aura-time` [feature flag](./feature-flags.md#custom-menu).
