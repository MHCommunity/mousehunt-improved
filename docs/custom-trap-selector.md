# [Custom Trap Selector](https://www.mousehuntgame.com/preferences.php?tab=mousehunt-improved-settings#mousehunt-improved-settings-custom-trap-selector)

Rearrange and hide the blocks under your trap, like the trap effectiveness, auras, catch rates, and codex.

Click **Edit trap selector** to open the editor. Drag the blocks in the preview or the list, or use the arrows, to reorder them, and use the toggles to show or hide them. The preview shows your trap as it looked the last time you were at camp. Changes are saved as you make them.

## Auras

The auras block lists your active auras and when they expire. Show them in a grid, as a list, or as icons only.

## Codex

Show the codex picker's images at the normal size or larger.
