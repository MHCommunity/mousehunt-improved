# [Favorite Setups](https://www.mousehuntgame.com/preferences.php?tab=mousehunt-improved-settings#mousehunt-improved-settings-favorite-setups)

Easily save and load your favorite trap setups. Click the "Favorite Setups" button under your trap on the Camp page to open the list. The button also shows the name of the setup you have armed.

## Features

- **Save Your Current Setup**: Your current setup is at the top of the list, with a button to save it.
- **Setups by Location**: Save a setup for a location, a whole region, or all locations. The list shows the setups for your current location, or switch to All to see every setup.
- **Arm in One Click**: Items you no longer have are highlighted. If you've run out of the bait or charm, the rest of the setup is still armed.
- **Edit Setups**: Change a setup's name, location, and items. Click the "Generate a name" button to get a name based on the setup's items and location.
- **Reorder**: Drag setups to change their order.
- **Top Menu**: Add Favorite Setups to the top menu with [Custom Menu](./custom-menu.md) to arm a setup from any page. Setups for your current location are listed first, or set it to only list those.

## Options

- **Include mobile favorites**: Also show the favorites saved in the mobile app.
- **Use generated names for setups**: Names new setups based on their items and location with AI. (Enabled by default)

> [!TIP]
> If you're looking for more features for favorite setups, check out the [Favorite Setups+](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/443164-mousehunt-favorite-setups) userscript.
