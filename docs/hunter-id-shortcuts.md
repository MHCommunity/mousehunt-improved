# [Hunter ID Shortcuts](https://www.mousehuntgame.com/preferences.php?tab=mousehunt-improved-settings#mousehunt-improved-settings-hunter-id-shortcuts)

Copy your Hunter ID from your profile picture on the left of the HUD stats bar, and paste a Hunter ID anywhere to open that hunter’s profile.

## Options

- **Copy your Hunter ID**: Show a "Copy ID" button when you hover over your profile picture on the left of the HUD stats bar, copy it when you click your profile picture, or turn it off.
- **Open a hunter’s profile when you paste their ID**: Press Ctrl/Cmd+V outside a text field with a Hunter ID or profile link copied to go straight to that hunter’s profile.
